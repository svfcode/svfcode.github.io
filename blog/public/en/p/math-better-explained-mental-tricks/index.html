Follow-up to Intuitive Math from BetterExplained — short tricks for back-of-the-envelope estimates.

1. Speed and reaction time

60 km/h is 1 km per minute.

A driver’s reaction time (notice → decide → act) is about 1 second. At 60 km/h that’s roughly 16 meters traveled in that second.

If an obstacle appears closer than ~16 m at 60 km/h, you probably cannot react in time.

Think about it: at three times the speed (180 km/h), what distance becomes critical?

2. Work hours in a year

1 year ≈ 250 work days ≈ 2000 work hours (250 × 8).

If you spend 1 hour a day commuting (30 minutes each way), that’s 250 hours per year — more than a full month of work time.
